# Bacon's Secret

## Description

My friend sent me this note. He said the **style** of the message is what matters, not the words.

Can you work out what he was really trying to tell me?

---

## Category
- **Cryptography / Classical Ciphers**

## Difficulty
- **Easy / Medium**

---

## Provided

- **secret.pdf** — the note from my friend

---

## Scenario

At first glance, the note reads like ordinary text. Nothing in the wording looks suspicious.

But the friend was very clear: **ignore what it says, look at how it's written.**

The real message is hiding in plain sight.

---

## Objective

Extract the hidden message from the note and recover the flag.

Flag format: `THG{flag}`

---

## Hints

- **Hint 1:** Look closely at how each letter is styled. Do you see two different kinds?
- **Hint 2:** Two styles means two symbols. Think binary, but older.
- **Hint 3:** The challenge name is a clue. Who was Bacon, and what did he invent?

---

## Goal

Decode the hidden message in the note's styling and recover the flag.
