# The Downtown Corner

## Description

An agent has provided this image from a busy city street.

Your challenge, should you choose to accept, is to identify the **main commercial building that is just out of frame, behind the photographer**.

---

## Category
- **OSINT / Geolocation**

## Difficulty
- **Easy / Medium**

---

## Provided

- **Intel.png** — the photo sent in by the agent

---

## Scenario

The photo shows a city street corner: office towers, shops, a tree-lined footpath and plenty of people passing by.

The building you're after isn't in the picture at all. It's **behind the camera**.

Work out exactly where the photo was taken, then turn around.

---

## Objective

Identify the name of the commercial building directly behind the photographer.

Flag format: `THG{Name_of_the_Place}`

---

## Hints

- **Hint 1:** Signs and text in the photo tell you more than you think. Read everything, including the ground.
- **Hint 2:** Once you know the street, the shops in the photo can confirm the exact corner.
- **Hint 3:** Street View lets you stand where the photographer stood and look the other way.

---

## Goal

Geolocate the photo, look behind the photographer, and name the building to recover the flag.
