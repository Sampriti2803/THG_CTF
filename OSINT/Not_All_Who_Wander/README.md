# Not All Who Wander...

## Description

Kerani Nair's bio says, *"Not all those who wander are lost,"* but her family fears she might be.

She was so excited about her first solo trip, but she hasn't been picking up calls or returned back to her hostel. Her **Instagram** is all we have.

According to her digital footprint on Instagram, **where was she last seen?**

---

## Category
- **OSINT**

## Difficulty
- **Easy / Medium**

---

## Scenario

All you have to go on:

- Her name: **Kerani Nair**
- Her bio: *"Not all those who wander are lost"*
- The fact that she was on her **first solo trip**

Everything she shared online is still out there. Follow her trail and figure out the last place she was seen.

---

## Objective

Identify the exact location where Kerani was last seen and submit its **Google Maps Place ID**.

Flag format: `THG{PlaceID_Of_Location}`

---

## Hints

- **Hint 1:** Her bio isn't the only thing on her profile. Check posts, captions, tagged locations and stories/highlights.
- **Hint 2:** Look closely at the background details in her photos.
- **Hint 3:** Google Maps gives every place a unique Place ID. Try the Place ID Finder.

---

## Goal

Trace Kerani's digital footprint, pinpoint where she was last seen, and recover the flag.
