# The Apprentice's Folly

## Description

The Grand Wizard is locked out of his own portal! His over-eager apprentice tried to *"modernize"* the login system.

> *"Bah! He said he hid my 'secret incantation' right on the page, claiming it's the only way to prove I'm me! He kept muttering about 'inspecting' things and 'viewing sources'... nonsense! All I know is, only true administrators are allowed in the inner sanctum, and now I can't get in!"*

Can you find the apprentice's **hidden incantation** and discover the path to the **inner sanctum**?

## Scenario

You'll find:

- A login portal for the Grand Wizard
- A **secret incantation** hidden somewhere on the page
- An **inner sanctum** that only administrators can enter

The apprentice thought hiding the secret on the page was clever. It wasn't.

---

## Objective

Find the hidden incantation, prove you're an administrator, and reach the inner sanctum.

Flag format: `THG{flag}`

---

## Hints

- **Hint 1:** The Wizard's muttering is a clue. What does "viewing sources" mean in a browser?
- **Hint 2:** Not everything on a page is visible on the page. Check the HTML and the scripts it loads.
- **Hint 3:** If the site decides who's an admin, check how it remembers who you are.

---

## Goal

Uncover the apprentice's hidden incantation and find your way into the inner sanctum to recover the flag.
