## Scenario

The intern rebuilt the website and threw away the old one. Their time here was short, but the internet doesn't forget so easily.

All you have is the current site and the intern's riddle. Every line of it points somewhere.

Unlock the **/sanctum**.

---

## Objective

Follow the riddle, recover the intern's two-part key, and get past the /sanctum.

Flag format: `THG{flag}`

---

## Hints

- **Hint 1:** Websites tell crawlers where not to go. That file is worth reading.
- **Hint 2:** The old halls are gone from the live site, but someone may have archived them.
- **Hint 3:** Read the riddle line by line. Each line is a clue.

---

## Goal

Decode the riddle, dig up the old version of the site, and unlock the /sanctum to recover the flag.
